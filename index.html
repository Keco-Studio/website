What is Keco Studio?

Keco Studio is an AI-native workspace for game designers. It brings your mechanics, numbers and content into one connected system, so you can design, test and balance your game before development starts.

Who is Keco for?

Game designers, narrative teams and technical designers, from solo creators to full studios. You don’t need to write code to use it.

Can Keco really turn a GDD into a playable game?

Yes. Import or write your game design document and Keco structures the rules, numbers and content inside it, then generates a playable prototype you can test and iterate on.

What can the Keco agent do?

It works directly on your project: editing and rebalancing tables, generating art assets, drafting design docs, and converting spreadsheets or scripts into clean, linked data.

What is MCP and why does it matter?

MCP (Model Context Protocol) lets language models connect to Keco. They can read your design docs and write changes straight into your game engine without any code.

Which formats and engines are supported?

Keco imports CSV and XLSX, and exports JSON, CSV and custom schemas. Exporters for Godot and Unity are available, with more engines on the way.

Is Keco free to use?

The Starter plan is free and includes the core tools. Pro and Studio add AI agent credits, collaboration and priority support. See Pricing for details.
