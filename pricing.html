FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about billing or a custom plan?

Get in touch with the team
What are agent credits?

Agent credits are used each time the Keco AI agent does work for you, such as generating tables, art assets or design docs. Simple edits use fewer credits; larger generations use more.

What happens if I run out of credits?

Your projects and data stay fully available. AI agent features pause until your credits reset at the start of your next billing cycle, or you can upgrade your plan at any time.

Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your account settings. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period.

Do unused credits roll over?

Credits reset each month and don’t roll over. Studio and Enterprise teams can contact us about pooled or annual credit options.

Are Marketplace purchases included in my plan?

No. Templates, components and plugins on the Marketplace are bought separately, and many are free. Once purchased, they’re yours to use in any project.

Do you offer discounts for students or indie teams?

Yes. Students, educators and small indie teams can get discounted Pro plans. Contact us with a few details about your project.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards. Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice.
