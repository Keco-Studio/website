FAQ

Questions from Game Designers

Something else on your mind?

Get in touch with the team
Do I need to know how to code?

No. You design mechanics, tables and balance rules visually or in plain language. Keco handles the structure and exports for your engine.

Can I import my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. Keco imports CSV and XLSX files and keeps references between tables intact, so an item’s loot table or a unit’s skills stay linked.

How does balance simulation work?

Keco runs thousands of simulated sessions using your real data and formulas, then charts progression, difficulty and economy curves so you can spot problems early.

Can the AI agent change my data?

Only when you ask. Every agent change is shown for review and saved as a version snapshot, so you can compare or roll back at any time.

Which genres does Keco work for?

Any game with systems behind it: RPGs, roguelikes, strategy, card games, simulations and more. Marketplace templates give you a head start in each.
